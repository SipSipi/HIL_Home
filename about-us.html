About Us

A Brief Story About the Company

Founded in the heart of Kelantan, HIL Home was built on a simple belief: every great nation begins with strong homes, strong communities, and strong values.

We are a construction and development company specialising in Design & Build, new home construction, renovations, extensions, and property development. From the first concept to project completion, we are committed to delivering quality workmanship, innovative solutions, and exceptional customer service.

"Construction is not just about building houses. It is about creating opportunities, strengthening families, developing communities, and leaving a legacy for future generations."

Driven by Islamic values, professional management, and a passion for excellence, we strive to become one of the region's most respected construction companies — trusted by our clients, valued by our partners, and recognised for making a meaningful difference in society.
